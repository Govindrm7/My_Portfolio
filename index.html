01 / About

Demos are easy. Production is the job.

Most GenAI work dies somewhere between proof of concept and production, and the gap is rarely the model. It's everything around it. Retrieval quality you can measure instead of eyeball. Hallucination rates you track instead of hope about. Guardrails, cost and latency budgets, traces you can actually debug from. Closing that gap is what I do at Pfizer, where I build LangGraph agents, multimodal RAG and MCP tooling, plus the evaluation and observability that keep them honest.

I'm a software engineer as much as an ML one. I came up through backend work (Java, Spring Boot and REST APIs at BMC Software), trained PyTorch vision models as a research assistant, and now build full-stack products end to end: FastAPI services, React front ends, Docker and Kubernetes on AWS. Alongside it I write research, with seven-plus peer-reviewed papers across medical imaging, document intelligence and multi-agent systems.
